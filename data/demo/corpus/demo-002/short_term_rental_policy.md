# Minneapolis Home — Short-Term Rental (Airbnb) Policy Summary

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. A fictional summary of how a short-term rental is commonly
> regulated at three layers — city, association, and insurer — written for a class
> capstone demo. It quotes no real ordinance or statute and is not legal advice.

Applies to: **1412 Larkspur Lane, Minneapolis, MN 55409** (the primary home), for
the **owner** acting as host. The Dallas, TX home is covered by its own policy — the
two communities set different minimum stays, so do not apply this one to it.

## Three Layers Have To Agree
A short-term rental is only permitted when **all three** of these allow it. Any one
of them can stop the rental on its own:

1. **The city** — registration, licensing, safety inspection, and lodging tax.
2. **The homeowners association** — the Lakeshore Commons CC&Rs.
3. **The insurer** — a standard homeowner policy usually does not cover paid guests.

Owners commonly discover the second and third only after listing. Check all three
before accepting a booking.

## 1. City Registration and Licence
Hosting short-term stays generally requires registering the property with the city
and displaying the registration number in the listing. Registration typically
requires the owner's name and contact details, a local responsible party who can
respond within a short window, a basic life-safety self-certification, and an annual
fee.

Whole-home rentals and owner-occupied room rentals are often treated as different
licence categories, with the whole-home category carrying more conditions.

## 2. Lodging Tax
Stays under 30 days are usually subject to a lodging or transient-occupancy tax.
Large booking platforms often collect and remit some of these automatically, but
**not always all of them** — an owner is generally still responsible for any portion
the platform does not remit. Keep the platform's remittance statements.

## 3. The Association's Rule — Minimum 30 Days
The Lakeshore Commons CC&Rs set a **minimum lease term of 30 days** (see the
leasing section of the CC&Rs). A booking shorter than 30 days is therefore a
covenant violation regardless of whether the city would licence it.

This is the binding constraint at this home. The practical consequence: nightly and
weekend Airbnb-style hosting is **not permitted** here. Stays of 30 days or longer —
corporate housing, travelling professionals, a between-homes tenancy — are permitted
and are the usual way owners in this community let their homes.

Owners must give the association the tenant's name and contact details within 14
days of any lease starting, including a 30-day-plus stay.

## 4. Insurance
A standard homeowner policy commonly excludes business use, and paid lodging is
business use. Options are a short-term-rental endorsement, a landlord policy for
longer tenancies, or a dedicated host policy. Platform-provided host protection is
generally secondary coverage and is not a substitute for telling your insurer.

Tell the insurer in writing before the first paid stay. A claim denied for
undisclosed business use is the expensive version of this lesson.

## 5. Safety Requirements Commonly Attached to a Licence
- Working smoke alarms on every level and in every sleeping room.
- A carbon-monoxide alarm within ten feet of each sleeping room — this home has gas
  heat, a gas range and a gas water heater, so CO detection is not optional.
- A fire extinguisher in the kitchen.
- Posted egress information and an emergency contact number.
- Unobstructed egress windows in any basement sleeping room. The finished basement
  in this home counts here.

## 6. Winter-Specific Host Duties
Cold-climate hosting adds duties a warm-climate listing does not have:
- Snow and ice must be cleared from the driveway, steps and public sidewalk,
  typically within 24 hours after snowfall ends. Arrange this before the season, not
  during a storm — guests will not do it.
- Guests must be told not to turn the heat off. Leave written instructions and set a
  minimum thermostat setting.
- Leave the water main shutoff location marked and the local responsible party's
  number visible.

## 7. Record-Keeping
Keep the registration certificate, the insurance endorsement, tax remittance
statements, guest records, and any written association correspondence. If the
association or the city questions a stay, the records are the defence.
