# Home Appliance & Maintenance Care Guide

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. A fictional homeowner care guide for a class capstone demo,
> summarizing widely published maintenance practices. Not manufacturer instructions;
> always follow the manual for your specific model.

## HVAC Air Filters
Replace (or clean, if reusable) the HVAC air filter regularly to protect the system and
air quality. A common schedule is every **90 days** for a standard 1-inch pleated filter,
shortened to **30–60 days** with pets, allergies, or heavy use. Thicker media filters
(4–5 inch) can last 6–12 months. Match the filter size printed on the old filter (for this
home, 16x25x1) and the airflow arrow toward the blower. A clogged filter raises energy use
and can cause the system to freeze up or short-cycle.

## Refrigerator Care
Vacuum the condenser coils every 6–12 months so the compressor runs efficiently. Keep the
fridge at about 37°F and the freezer at 0°F. Replace water/ice filters roughly every 6
months. Check and clean the door gasket so it seals.

## Dishwasher Care
Clean the filter at the bottom monthly, wipe the door seal, and run a cleaning cycle with a
dishwasher cleaner monthly to prevent odor and buildup. Do not overload; check spray arms
for clogs.

## Washer and Dryer Care
Clean the dryer **lint trap every load** and the dryer vent duct at least yearly — lint
buildup is a common house-fire cause. For washers, leave the door open to dry, and run a
tub-clean cycle monthly on front-loaders to prevent mildew.

## Water Heater Maintenance
Drain a few gallons from the tank yearly to flush sediment. Test the temperature-and-
pressure relief valve annually. Setting the thermostat to about 120°F saves energy and
reduces scald risk. Keep the area around a gas unit clear and well ventilated.

## Mounting and Anchoring Items on Walls
The safe method depends on the item's **weight** and the **wall type**:
- **Into a wood stud:** strongest option. A screw driven into a stud can hold heavy items
  (mirrors, shelves, TVs). Use a stud finder; studs are usually 16 inches apart.
- **Hollow drywall (no stud):** use rated anchors. Plastic expansion anchors hold only a
  few pounds; **self-drilling or toggle/molly bolts** are needed for heavier loads (a
  20-lb mirror needs anchors rated well above 20 lb, ideally toggle bolts, or mounting into
  a stud). Never hang heavy items on a plain nail in drywall.
- **Masonry/brick/concrete:** use a masonry bit and sleeve/wedge anchors.
- **Renters / no-drill:** heavy-duty adhesive strips and hanging rails avoid holes but have
  strict weight limits — check the package rating and the surface type.
Always confirm the anchor's rated weight exceeds the item's weight with a safety margin, and
spread the load across two points for wide items.

## Seasonal Maintenance Checklist
- **Every 90 days:** replace the HVAC filter; test smoke/CO alarms.
- **Spring/Fall:** service the HVAC system; clean gutters; check the roof.
- **Before winter:** flush the water heater; winterize outdoor spigots and irrigation.
- **Annually:** clean the dryer vent; vacuum fridge coils; inspect caulking and weather-strip.
