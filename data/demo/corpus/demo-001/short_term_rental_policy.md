## What Counts as a Short-Term Rental
A short-term rental (STR) is any rental of a home, or part of a home, for fewer than
30 consecutive days. Listing a property on platforms such as Airbnb or VRBO for nightly
or weekly stays is a short-term rental and is subject to both HOA rules and city
ordinance.

## HOA Short-Term Rental Restrictions
Within Maple Grove, short-term rentals are permitted only if the owner:
- Registers the rental with the HOA and provides a local 24/7 contact person.
- Rents the entire home, not individual rooms to separate parties.
- Limits occupancy to two persons per bedroom plus two additional guests.
- Ensures guests follow community quiet hours (10 p.m. to 7 a.m.) and parking rules.
The HOA may suspend an owner's STR privileges after repeated documented violations by
guests. The owner remains responsible for all guest conduct.

## City STR Ordinance
The city requires anyone operating a short-term rental to:
- Obtain an annual STR registration/permit and post the permit number in every listing.
- Collect and remit the local hotel/occupancy tax on all stays.
- Provide a working smoke alarm in every bedroom, a carbon-monoxide alarm on each floor,
  and at least one fire extinguisher.
- Post an evacuation diagram and emergency contact information inside the unit.
Operating without a permit can result in fines and removal of the listing.

## Insurance Considerations
A standard homeowners policy often excludes commercial or short-term-rental activity.
Owners should confirm coverage with their insurer or obtain a short-term-rental
endorsement or separate policy before hosting guests.

## Owner Checklist Before Listing
1. Confirm the HOA allows STRs and complete HOA registration.
2. Obtain the city STR permit and note the permit number.
3. Set up occupancy-tax collection and remittance.
4. Install and test smoke/CO alarms and fire extinguishers; post the evacuation diagram.
5. Confirm short-term-rental insurance coverage.
6. Configure the listing's house rules to match HOA quiet hours, occupancy, and parking.
