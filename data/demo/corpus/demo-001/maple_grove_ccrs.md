# Maple Grove HOA — Covenants, Conditions & Restrictions (CC&Rs)

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. This is a fictional homeowners-association document created
> for a class capstone demo. It does not describe any real HOA and is not legal
> advice. Section numbers are illustrative.

Applies to: **5000 Maple Street, Dallas, TX 75201** (the secondary home). The
Minneapolis, MN home is governed by a different association — do not apply these
covenants to it.

## 1. Purpose and Applicability
These covenants apply to all lots within the Maple Grove community. Every owner
agrees to comply with these rules and with decisions of the Architectural Review
Committee (ARC). Tenants and short-term guests are bound by the same rules, and the
owner is responsible for their compliance.

## 2. Architectural Review Committee (ARC) Approval
Any exterior change to a home or lot that is visible from the street or from a
neighboring lot requires **written ARC approval before work begins**. Owners submit
a request form with a description, materials, and a simple drawing or photo. The ARC
responds within 30 days. Work started without approval may be required to be reversed
at the owner's expense.

## 3. Exterior Modifications
Repainting in an approved color, replacing a roof with a like-for-like material, and
routine repairs do not require approval. Changes to paint color, roofing material,
additions, exterior lighting, solar panels, and antennas/satellite dishes larger than
one meter require ARC approval under Section 2.

## 4. Landscaping
### 4.1 General
Front and side yards must be kept maintained and free of weeds and debris. Dead plants
and trees should be replaced within 60 days.

### 4.2 Rock, Gravel, and Xeriscaping
Replacing a lawn with rock, gravel, pebbles, or drought-tolerant (xeriscape)
landscaping **is permitted, but requires ARC approval** of a design plan. Approved
plans typically require that living plants or ground cover make up at least 40% of the
front-yard area; pure rock or gravel front yards are generally not approved. Backyards
that are not visible from the street have more flexibility and usually only need
approval if drainage to neighboring lots is affected.

### 4.3 Trees
Removing a healthy tree with a trunk diameter over 6 inches requires ARC approval.

### 4.4 Artificial Turf
Artificial turf is allowed in backyards with ARC approval and is generally not approved
for front yards.

## 5. Pools, Spas, and Water Features
In-ground and above-ground pools, spas, and hot tubs require **ARC approval and all
applicable city/county permits** (see the city permit checklist). Pool equipment must
be screened from view and comply with community quiet hours. Fencing around a pool must
meet the safety-barrier requirements in the city building code.

## 6. Sheds and Accessory Structures
Storage sheds and accessory structures require ARC approval, may not exceed 120 square
feet or 8 feet in height, must be placed in the rear yard, and must use exterior colors
matching the home.

## 7. Fences and Walls
Fences require ARC approval. Approved materials are wood, wrought iron, or approved
composite. Front-yard fences may not exceed 4 feet; rear/side fences may not exceed 6
feet. Chain-link fencing is not permitted in areas visible from the street.

## 8. Parking and Vehicles
Vehicles must be parked in garages or driveways. Recreational vehicles, boats, and
trailers may not be stored in view for more than 48 hours. Commercial vehicles over
one ton may not be parked overnight.

## 9. Short-Term Rentals
Short-term rentals (for example, listing a home on Airbnb or VRBO) are subject to the
community's Short-Term Rental Policy and to city ordinances. See the separate
Short-Term Rental Policy document. In brief, rentals shorter than 30 days require
registration with the HOA and compliance with the city's STR permit process.

## 10. Fines and Enforcement
The HOA may issue a written notice of violation with a cure period, typically 14 days.
Unresolved violations may result in fines starting at $50 and increasing for repeat
offenses. Owners may appeal to the board within 30 days of a notice.
