# Dallas Home — Renter / Tenant Policy Summary (Sample)

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. A fictional, general summary of common landlord–tenant
> practices for a class capstone demo. This is not legal advice; tenant law varies
> by state and city. Renters should check their lease and local law.

## Security Deposits
Landlords commonly collect a security deposit of one to two months' rent. After
move-out, the landlord typically must return the deposit, minus documented deductions
for damage beyond normal wear and tear, within a set period (often 14–30 days) along
with an itemized statement. Photos at move-in and move-out help resolve disputes.

## Repairs and Maintenance
Landlords are generally responsible for keeping the unit habitable: working heat,
plumbing, electrical, and structural safety. Tenants are usually responsible for
keeping the unit clean, reporting problems promptly, and paying for damage they cause.
Repair requests should be made in writing and dated.

## Alterations by the Tenant
Unless the lease allows it, tenants usually may not make permanent alterations —
painting, drilling large holes, mounting heavy fixtures, or changing locks — without
the landlord's written permission. Small nail holes for hanging pictures are often
allowed; always check the lease. Unapproved alterations may be deducted from the
security deposit.

## Landlord Entry and Notice
Landlords generally must give advance notice (commonly 24 hours) before entering for
non-emergency reasons such as repairs or inspections, and may enter without notice only
for genuine emergencies like a burst pipe or fire.

## Lease Termination
Fixed-term leases end on the stated date; month-to-month tenancies usually require
written notice (often 30 days) from either party. Breaking a lease early may make the
tenant responsible for rent until the unit is re-rented, subject to the landlord's duty
to try to re-rent.

## Documenting Problems (for Renters)
Keep written records: dated repair requests, photos of issues, and copies of all
communication with the landlord. This documentation supports maintenance requests and
helps defend a security deposit at move-out.
